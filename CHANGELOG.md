# AltBridge changelog

What changed in each version. [Download the latest AltBridge](https://github.com/kushagragupta126/altbridge-updates/raw/main/download/AltBridge.dmg).

## 1.3.5 · 28 Oct 2026

- AltBridge keeps hearing your keys after your Mac has sat idle, slept or locked for hours: it no longer naps, and it restarts its keyboard listener after wake and unlock
- If macOS Secure Input is hiding your keys (often left on after unlocking), AltBridge now says so and how to clear it

## 1.3.4 · 16 Oct 2026

- Key tips, learning hints and notices now show over Excel in full screen, also with Stage Manager and two displays

## 1.3.3 · 15 Oct 2026

- Key tips always appear on the workbook you're working in, even with several Excel windows, two screens or one in full screen
- What's New: every version's changes in Settings, and a note after each update with a See What's New button

## 1.3.2 · 14 Oct 2026

- No more keychain password prompts about "app.altbridge.trial" after updating

## 1.3.1 · 12 Oct 2026

- Key tips now show on every screen: with more than one display, the badges were drawn on the wrong screen and stayed invisible

## 1.3 · 11 Oct 2026

- Report a Problem now goes to altbridge.support@gmail.com
- Anonymous usage counts (a random ID, the version, trial or licensed), with an off switch in Settings › About

## 1.2 · 11 Oct 2026

- A 30-day free trial: AltBridge works straight away, then asks for a licence key
- Report a Problem now goes straight to the developer's email

## 1.1.1 · 11 Oct 2026

- Lifetime licences: keys that never end show as "Lifetime" in Settings › About
- The read-me in the download now covers entering your licence key

## 1.1 · 11 Oct 2026

- Licence keys: AltBridge asks for the key from your invite, and beta keys show how many days are left
- Your licence is in the menu-bar panel and in Settings › About
- A gentle reminder in a beta's last week
- If a shortcut needs a key, AltBridge says so where you pressed it, with an Enter Key button

## 1.0.1 · 11 Oct 2026

- ⌥ H V V (paste values) works again
- ⌥ H B A (all borders) and other menu shortcuts no longer report success when nothing happened
- Shortcuts that open a menu find it straight away, without a one-second pause
- If a menu step fails, AltBridge's backup route now runs properly

## 1.0 · 9 Oct 2026

- AltBridge 1.0: the first official release
- Windows Excel Alt key sequences and key tips, on your Mac
- Updates arrive by themselves, signed and checked before they install
- Report a Problem: send what happened, with your versions and (if you choose) the activity log
- Learning hints show next to Excel's Share button and teach items inside ribbon menus
